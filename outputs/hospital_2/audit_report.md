## What limits this audit

- 1709 line occurrences have no resolved service identity. Each is priced as a set of possible services and is flagged only if its price is wrong under every reading. No invoice containing one gets a submitted corrected total.
- Those lines also widen cumulative utilisation, Service Day aggregates and bundle detection for every service they might be. Where that straddles a threshold, the affected lines accept any rate the plausible readings allow.
- When the semantic stage verifies a mapping, the next `audit h2` prices those lines exactly, and invoices with no other blocker become reconstructable. No manual step is needed.
- `artifacts/hospital_2/unresolved.json` lists every unresolved or unknown cluster by group; `outputs/hospital_2/decision_log.md` gives the reading behind each rule.
