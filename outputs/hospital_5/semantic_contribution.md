## Jev missing-word review

33 clusters (identity key × billed unit basis) covering 1042 lines, one question each. Raw choices: {'specific_service': 31, 'ambiguous_contracted_service': 2, 'none_of_the_above_or_unknown': 0}. Gated outcomes: {'ambiguous_contracted': 1, 'service': 28, 'unresolved': 4}.

A service is accepted only when Jev chose it with P >= 0.90 and confidence >= 0.80, and the description evidences all but at most one of its name slots. A single-candidate closure rule (which counted Jev's mass on `ambiguous_contracted_service` as support for the only candidate) was tried after the first run and removed in review: with one candidate that mass is the reviewer hedging over the missing word, not confirming the service. The four clusters it would have accepted stay unresolved (see the decision log).

One cluster was closed to its two contracted candidates, and the unit basis then separates them (the tie-break after review). Without it: reconstructable invoices 884, identified lines 13045.

## Human-reviewed overrides

Neither is global, and no closure rule is involved: the descriptions they resolve carry the discriminator, abbreviated. Without the file: reconstructable invoices 706, identified lines 12861 (FINAL: 939 and 13113).

## Financial equivalence

Stage 4 -> 5: reconstructable invoices +3; 0 lines are semantically ambiguous but financially resolved; 0 submitted totals contain such a line. Financial equivalence also removes blanks caused by uncertainty that does not reach the amount: a malformed date on a service whose price does not depend on the day (or whose possible days all price the same), and a possible repeat, trigger or partner that every reading prices the same.

Measured without the Jev missing-word stage (where ambiguous lines remain), financial equivalence moves reconstructable invoices from 385 to 386 and resolves 0 ambiguous lines financially: without a review, every ambiguous line keeps an uncontracted reading, which has no contract price. Even if every ambiguous line were treated as certainly contracted, its candidates would price identically on 0 of the 68 ambiguous lines that have two or more candidates (a line with one candidate trivially agrees with itself and is not counted). Structural candidates differ in base rate, multipliers or unit basis, so equivalence across services is rare in this contract; the mechanism is kept because it is correct, not because it is productive here.
